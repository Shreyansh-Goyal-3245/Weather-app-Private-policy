Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Weather ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Location Information

The App may access your device's approximate or precise location in order to provide local weather information and forecasts.

User Inputs

If you use AI-powered weather features, any prompts or questions entered into the App may be sent to Google's Gemini services to generate weather-related responses.

Account and Cloud Data

If cloud features are enabled, account information and app-related data may be stored and processed using Supabase services.

Diagnostic Information

The App may collect limited technical information necessary to monitor stability, diagnose issues, improve performance, and maintain service reliability.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide weather forecasts and weather-related functionality.
  • Generate AI-powered weather summaries and responses.
  • Maintain user accounts and cloud synchronization features.
  • Improve application performance and reliability.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • WeatherAPI
  • Google Gemini
  • Supabase

These services may process information necessary to provide functionality. Your use of the App may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of these service providers.

4. Data Security

Information transmitted between the App and supported services is intended to use secure HTTPS/TLS connections whenever available.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality, maintain user accounts, improve services, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

6. User Choices

Users may:

  • Deny location permissions through device settings.
  • Disable cloud-sync features when available.
  • Remove stored app data through device settings.
  • Uninstall the App at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If information is believed to have been collected improperly, please contact the developer.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes become effective when the updated version is published.

9. Contact Information

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official support contact provided with the application.
